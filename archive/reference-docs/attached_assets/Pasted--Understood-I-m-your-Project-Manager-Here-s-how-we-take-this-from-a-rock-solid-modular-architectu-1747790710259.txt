## \*\*1. **Immediate Next Sprint: Integration, Type Safety & Data Flow**

**Goal:** Eliminate all TypeScript errors, ensure all modules talk seamlessly, and set the stage for real data.

### **Tasks:**

* Review all cross-module interfaces and props.
