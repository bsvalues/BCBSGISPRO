# 🟢 **PM/CTO Endgame Checklist — Musk Demo Dashboard**

---

## 1. **Data Quality & Health Panel**

* **Display a “Data Integrity” or “Parcel Cleanliness” score**—big, bold, and visible.
* Show live count of valid/invalid parcels, flagged anomalies, and time since last health check.
* Add a “Run Data Health Check” button—admin can trigger a validation scan right from the dashboard.

---

## 2. **AI “What’s Important?” Callout**

* Top-center, a “What should I care about?” box.

  * AI summarizes:

    * “No issues. All systems nominal.”
    * “2 parcels have unresolved anomalies: click to review.”
* **Let Musk/board see that AI is truly assisting, not just explaining.**

---

## 3. **Audit Trail and “Explain This”**

* Next to each timeline event, an “Explain This” button.

  * Clicking calls the AI summary for that event, shown inline.
  * Example: “This document was uploaded outside of normal workflow hours. AI suggests confirming authenticity.”

---

## 4. **Webhook/API Live Status**

* Real-time “heartbeat” indicator for webhook/API demo.
* Show “last 5 webhook calls,” with success/failure, right on the dashboard.

---

## 5. **Metrics Cards with Micro-Details**

* Each metric card (Workflows, Health, Events, Hours Saved) opens on hover/tap to show:

  * Who last touched it
  * Last anomaly
  * Average time-to-close (for workflows)
* No clutter—just drilldown on demand.

---

## 6. **Manual Data Correction Flow**

* On any flagged parcel or anomaly, allow the admin to click “Review & Correct.”
* Opens a modal with full history, AI suggestion, and “Approve Fix”/“Flag for Review.”

---

## 7. **Zero-Error, Zero-Downtime Guarantee**

* Add a tiny “System Uptime: 100%” and “Error Log: 0 critical, 0 warnings” footer.
* If any error is detected, make it obvious, but not scary—think Tesla “service required” vibe.
