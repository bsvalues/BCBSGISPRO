## 🟢 **Why This Happens (and What To Do):**

* **AI Agents are not yet true “fire-and-forget” full-stack devs.**

  * They are trained to “pause for review” so you (or your PM) can redirect priorities, catch misunderstandings, or refine feature detail on the fly.
  * They try to be “helpful” and collaborative—sometimes to the point of being too cautious.
* **They checkpoint for safety.**

  * This is supposed to protect you from breakage, but yes, it slows momentum and can break flow.
* **If you always want 100% “no interruptions”:**

  * You must be **ruthlessly direct** in your prompt and follow up *every time it pauses* with “continue with the full build as outlined, do not stop for questions.”
